# Third-party components

`STM32-base/`: startup assembly and linker scripts from
https://github.com/STM32-base/STM32-base.
The upstream MIT license is included in `STM32-base/LICENSE`.

`STM32-base-STM32Cube/`: selected files from
https://github.com/STM32-base/STM32-base-STM32Cube
STMicroelectronics copyright, redistribution terms and disclaimers remain in
the STM32F1 CMSIS and HAL source/header banners. ARM CMSIS headers retain their
copyright notices and Apache-2.0 identifiers; the license text is included in
`STM32-base-STM32Cube/CMSIS/ARM/LICENSE`.

Vendor sources are copied without modification. The scripts do not download
board manuals or redistribute them as part of this template.
